Three Components of Attitude

Understanding how people think, feel and act

Definition

Attitude is a person’s way of thinking, feeling and behaving towards a person, object or situation.

Cognitive and Affective Components

psychology

Cognitive Component

Think

Refers to beliefs, thoughts and opinions.

Workplace example

“I believe teamwork improves productivity.”

Affective Component

Feel

Refers to emotions and feelings.

Workplace example

“I feel happy working with my team.”

Part A · Attitude · 3 of 4

Behavioural Component — ACT

directions_walkAct

Refers to a person’s tendency or intention to behave in a particular way.

Workplace example

“I actively help my teammates and participate in projects.”

balance

Behaviour reflects attitude, but not always

Actions may not match what a person thinks or feels, for example helping only because a manager is watching.

Scenario: an employee working in a team

THINKCognitive

“Teamwork helps us achieve better results.”

FEELAffective

“I enjoy working with my colleagues.”

“I cooperate with my team and contribute ideas.”

Remember

COGNITIVE = THINK

AFFECTIVE = FEEL

BEHAVIOURAL = ACT

Why It Matters

Attitude can influence workplace relationships, cooperation and performance.

Employee Engagement

Beyond Just Working for a Salary

Definition

Employee engagement is the level of emotional commitment, involvement and dedication an employee has towards their work and organization.
